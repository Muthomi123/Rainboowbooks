About Rainbow Books

Founded in 1946, Rainbow Books is a Melbourne institution focused on quality bookselling and publishing. This prototype site demonstrates a possible digital transformation: blending e-commerce, community events, and author services.

Our Mission

To connect readers and authors through curated, human-led book experiences — online and in-store.
